# Rhetoric Desktop

The teleprompter that sits right under your Mac's camera, so you can read your script and still look into the lens. From [Rhetoric](https://rhetoricspeech.com).

**[Download for Mac](https://github.com/rhetoricspeech/rhetoric-desktop/releases/latest/download/Rhetoric-Desktop-arm64.dmg)**  
Apple silicon · macOS 13 or later · signed and notarized

## Install

1. Open the downloaded file and drag **Rhetoric Desktop** into **Applications**.
2. Open it from Applications. Rhetoric Desktop lives in your menu bar, not in the Dock: look for three stacked bars, widest at the top.
3. Click that icon, choose **Settings…**, then **Connect to Rhetoric**, and sign in to your Rhetoric account in your browser.

## Early preview

Rhetoric Desktop reads a built-in sample script for now. Your own speeches arrive in an update.
